One portrait, loaded once

Simulated Earth–Mars link

Each transmission carries a ZIP with audio and facial motion. The receiver blends the animated face into the shared still. The Mars videos show the resulting full frame. The H.264 comparison targets 64 kbps and uses the same crop, frame rate, and compressed speech as its ZIP.
